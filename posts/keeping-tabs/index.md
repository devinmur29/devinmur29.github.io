I've been meaning to start blogging for a while now ... and here we are! My appreciation for these corners of the internet started in high school when I discovered [Wait but Why](https://waitbutwhy.com/). Then when I began applying to college, I got in the habit of religiously checking the [MIT Admissions Blogs](https://mitadmissions.org/blogs/category/best-of-the-blogs/). As I finish up my M.Eng degree (7 years later 🤯) and prepare to enter a Ph.D program, I'm reading lots from academics at all stages of their career ([Sandra Bae](https://sandrabae.github.io/musings.html), [Matt Might](https://matt.might.net/articles/how-to-blog-as-an-academic/)).

In particular, Matt's advice convinced me to finally try my hand at keeping a blog, and I'm going to give it a shot over the next few months. ChatGPT recommended the [11ty framework](https://www.11ty.dev/) which has made it super easy, and now I have no excuse.

I have lots of ideas for future posts, but for this one I'd like to set some intentions. 

* I want to practice writing, so rest-assured dear reader all words written here are mine.

* I want to document new things I've learned, even (especially?) if nothing remarkable came of it.

* I want to reflect on career, but also

* I want to share what I'm up to *outside* of work.


As this new corner of the internet is birthed, I'll leave you with some others I revisit from time to time
